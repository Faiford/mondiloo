Mondiloo · Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · ← Back to Mondiloo

English

Mondiloo is a learning and play app for children ages 3 to 8. We built it so children can play safely.

  • No accounts and no personal information. Mondiloo does not ask for a name, email, photo, location, or any other personal information from children or parents.
  • Everything stays on the device. The chosen language, age group, sound setting and stars are saved only in the browser on your device. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data erases them.
  • No ads and no tracking. Mondiloo shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Voice. When sound is on, words are read aloud by your device's built-in voice.
  • Microphone. Only the "Say it with me" activity uses the microphone, and only after your device asks your permission and the child taps 🎤. The recording is kept for a few seconds in the app's memory so the child can hear their own voice. It is never saved, never sent anywhere, and it is erased when the child leaves the activity. We never hear it.
  • Fonts. The app loads its letter styles from Google Fonts the first time it opens. Like any website, Google receives your device's IP address for that request. After that, the fonts are stored on your device.

If this changes, for example if we add parent accounts, we will update this page and ask for a parent's permission first.

Kreyòl

Mondiloo se yon app pou timoun 3 a 8 an jwe epi aprann. Nou fè l pou timoun yo ka jwe an sekirite.

  • Pa gen kont, pa gen enfòmasyon pèsonèl. Mondiloo pa mande non, imèl, foto, kote w ye, ni okenn lòt enfòmasyon pèsonèl, ni bay timoun ni bay paran.
  • Tout bagay rete sou aparèy ou. Lang, laj, son ak zetwal yo sove sèlman nan navigatè ki sou aparèy ou a. Nou pa ka wè yo. Si ou efase done navigatè a, yo efase.
  • Pa gen piblisite, pa gen swivi. Mondiloo pa montre piblisite e li pa itilize okenn zouti pou swiv moun.
  • Vwa. Lè son an limen, se vwa ki deja nan aparèy ou a ki li mo yo.
  • Mikwofòn. Se sèlman aktivite "Repete apre m" la ki itilize mikwofòn nan, e sèlman apre aparèy ou mande w pèmisyon epi timoun nan peze 🎤. Anrejistreman an rete kèk segonn nan memwa app la pou timoun nan ka tande pwòp vwa li. Li pa janm sove, li pa janm voye okenn kote, e li efase lè timoun nan kite aktivite a. Nou pa janm tande l.
  • Lèt yo (polis). Premye fwa app la louvri, li chaje estil lèt yo sou Google Fonts. Menm jan ak nenpòt sit, Google resevwa adrès IP aparèy ou pou sa. Apre sa, lèt yo rete sou aparèy ou.

Si sa chanje, pa egzanp si nou ajoute kont pou paran, n ap mete paj sa a ajou epi n ap mande pèmisyon paran an anvan.
