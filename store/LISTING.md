**Full description** (max 4000)
Mondiloo is a joyful learning app for kids ages 3 to 8. Your child plays more than 50 short, colorful games and activities, in 4 languages: English, Haitian Creole (Kreyòl), French and Spanish. Switch language at any time.

PLAY AND LEARN
• Memory, mazes, coloring pages, puzzles, snake, balloons, tic-tac-toe, color sort, connect the dots, shadows, spot the difference and more, each with levels
• Words, letters, letter tracing, phrases, spelling, opposites
• "Say it with me": your child says a word and hears their own voice
• Counting to 100, adding, subtracting, times tables, patterns, missing numbers, clocks
• Discover the world: 11 countries, recipes from everywhere, animals, feelings and good habits
• Original songs and picture stories, including classic Haitian tales of Bouki and Malis
• Daily challenge with a streak, stars, trophies and a sticker album

MADE FOR EVERY AGE
Pick 3–4, 5–6 or 7–8 years and the activities adapt: bigger numbers, harder levels, new games.

FOR PARENTS
• A protected parent area with a screen-time limit and a weekly progress view
• Several child profiles on one device
• Printable worksheets in 4 languages

SAFE BY DESIGN
• No ads
• No accounts, no chat, no tracking
• Progress stays on your device
• Purchases and settings are behind a parent question
• Works offline

FREE AND PREMIUM
18 activities are free forever. Premium unlocks every activity, with a 7-day free trial.

Questions? contact@mondiloo.com · Privacy: https://mondiloo.com/privacy.html
